# Clubly Real-Content Onboarding Checklist

Use this checklist with every pilot club president and with Mrs. Rukkayat. Production content must be entered through authenticated Clubly screens; never run demo seed files in production.

## Before the session

- Confirm the club exists and the correct president is assigned in Members/User Management.
- Ask the president to bring an approved logo, at least one real activity photo, official social/contact links, dues information, and onboarding instructions.
- Confirm the president can sign in through CampusOne and reaches the President workspace.

## Club profile session

- Replace placeholder text with a specific club description.
- Select interest categories, skills students can build, career goals, meeting windows, and weekly commitment.
- Upload the approved logo and at least one real gallery image with consent.
- Add an official website or social link and the member-onboarding instructions.
- Confirm dues settings and the payment/onboarding path.
- Publish a real welcome announcement and add the next real event or proposal when available.

## Admin review

- Verify the president assignment, public visibility, and profile completeness checklist.
- Review the student-facing Discover Clubs card and detail page on mobile and desktop.
- Confirm there is no demo wording, fake activity, private WhatsApp information, or unapproved media.
- Record the reviewer, date, outstanding fields, and evidence in `Clubly bugs & fixes.xlsx`.

## Acceptance

A pilot club is ready only when its president is assigned, required profile fields are complete, at least one real announcement is visible, and a student can discover the club and understand how to join it.
